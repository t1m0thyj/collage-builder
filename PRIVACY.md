# Privacy

Your photos stay on your device. Collage Builder doesn't upload your photos, project contents, exports, or recovery drafts. You don't need an account, and the app has no analytics or advertising.

## What your browser keeps

For the website where you use Collage Builder, your browser stores:

- Photos needed for your project and previews used while editing.
- Up to five recent recovery drafts, within a total size limit of 2 GB.
- Preferences such as your layout and color settings.
- App files that let you return without an internet connection.

When you choose **Save** or **Export…**, the app writes a file to your device or downloads it through your browser. A linked project may ask for permission to read the photos you select. The wording of those permission prompts depends on your browser.

## Website requests

Like any website, the hosting provider receives information when your browser requests the app's files. This can include your IP address, browser information, requested address, and time of the request. The provider's privacy policy applies to those requests.

If you host your own copy, your hosting provider's policies apply. The app itself has no server that stores your photos or projects.

## Remove stored data

Click the save-status indicator in the top bar to manage recovery drafts. To remove all of the app's stored data, clear this website's data in your browser settings.

Clearing website data removes recovery drafts, preferences, and offline app files. Save a project file first if you want to keep your work. Files you've already saved or exported to your device are unaffected.

If you share a project or photo in a support request, check it for private information first.
